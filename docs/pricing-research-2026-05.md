# OpSolid Pricing Research — German Mid-Market B2B (2026-05)

> Pricing araştırması — research-scout agent çıktısı, 9 ürün × 3 tier matrix, rakip anchor'larıyla. Marketing teslimi öncesi pricing transparency için referans.

## Executive Summary

German Mittelstand expects transparent, EUR-denominated, GDPR-anchored pricing — not US-style "contact sales." Setup fees are normal here (anchors trust + filters tire-kickers). Per-usage on voice and WhatsApp is unavoidable (carrier passthrough); flat per-seat works for cards, chat, email, booking.

All prices **EUR ex-VAT**, EU-hosted. Setup = one-time; recurring = monthly unless noted.

## Cross-Product Rationale

**Why setup fees on every product:** German B2B expects them — signals commitment, funds onboarding labor, filters low-intent buyers. Removing setup = competing on price with Tidio/Cal.com/Wati = you lose. Setup also lets recurring stay lower (better LTV optics).

**Why per-usage on Voice/WhatsApp/Email but flat on Card/Chat/Booking:** Voice + WhatsApp have non-zero variable carrier cost (Twilio, Meta WABA) — flat exposes to abuse. Email overage optional. Cards/chat/booking near-zero marginal cost — flat per-seat cleaner to sell.

**Why "Professional" not "Pro":** German Mittelstand reads "Professional" as default mid-tier. "Pro" feels SaaS-startup; "Professional" feels Mittelstand-procurement-friendly.

**Bundle / discount strategy:**
- 2 products: -10% on combined recurring
- 3+ products: -15% on combined recurring + 1 free tier upgrade for 3 months
- Annual prepay: -15% (industry standard, frees cashflow)
- Verso teams (50+) bundled with any agent: Verso seat drops to €4 — wedge into bigger deals
- Custom Automation retainer customers: all SaaS at -20% — highest-loyalty cohort

**Pricing-page presentation (3-second test):**
1. EUR price ex-VAT for 3 tiers
2. Setup fee as separate line (not buried)
3. What's included as 4-5 bullets
4. "Talk to us" CTA on **Enterprise only** (Standard + Pro buy-now)
5. Same layout every product page — consistency over creativity
6. Avoid "From €X" — show actual tier prices
